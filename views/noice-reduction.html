Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free online noise reduction tool.

Is this noise reduction tool completely free?

Yes — Flarixo AI's noise reduction tool is 100% free with no sign-up or credit card required. Just upload and clean.

What audio formats are supported?

You can upload MP3, WAV, M4A, and most common audio formats for noise reduction.

Can I process multiple audio files at once?

Yes. Upload and clean multiple audio files in a single batch — processed files are delivered together as a ZIP archive.

How strong is the noise reduction?

Pick Light, Normal, or Strong to control how aggressively background noise, hiss, and static are removed from your recording.

Will noise reduction affect voice quality?

The Normal preset is tuned to remove background noise while preserving natural voice quality. Strong removes more noise but may slightly affect tone on heavily noisy recordings.

Are my uploaded audio files stored on your servers?

No. Files are processed securely in memory and immediately discarded after noise reduction. We never store, share, or access your files.
