FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about editing PDFs like Word documents with Flarixo AI.

Can I edit PDF text like a Word document?

Yes — that's exactly what Flarixo AI's PDF editor is built for. Our AI OCR engine detects every text region in your PDF and makes them clickable. When you click a word or paragraph, you can retype it and the editor automatically inherits the original font family, size, weight, color, and spacing. The result looks completely native — no mismatched overlays, no visible edits.

Does the editor match the original font and style after editing?

Yes. When OCR detects a text region, AI reads the apparent font properties — size, weight, italic, color, and letter spacing. When you click to edit, all those settings are pre-filled automatically. Your replacement text looks exactly like the original. You can override any property from the right panel if you want to change the style.

What is AI inpainting and how does it erase text?

AI inpainting is our clean-erase technology. When you select the AI Erase tool and drag over any text, logo, or image region, the AI analyzes the surrounding background — whether it's a solid color, gradient, pattern, or texture — and reconstructs it seamlessly. There are no white boxes, no artifacts, and no trace of what was there. The result looks like the content was never part of the document.

Can I change the background color of a PDF page?

Yes. The right panel has a dedicated Background section. Pick any color from the full HEX/HSL color picker and click Apply Background to instantly flood-fill the page background. This is great for changing white backgrounds to brand colors, dark mode conversions, or completely redesigning the visual layout of any PDF.

How does the color picker work?

Flarixo AI includes a native full-spectrum color picker with HEX and HSL support — that's over 16 million colors. You can set stroke color (for drawing and text), fill color (for shapes and fill tool), text color (synced from the right panel), and background color for the page. The picker opens inline when you click any color swatch in the toolbar or right panel.

Is sign-in required to use the PDF editor?

Yes — a free Flarixo AI account is required. Sign-in unlocks the full editor including OCR text detection, AI font matching, AI inpainting, the color picker, background color tool, freehand drawing, multi-page support, and export without watermarks. Creating an account takes under 30 seconds and requires no credit card.

Can I edit multi-page PDFs?

Yes. There is no limit on the number of pages. Use the Page navigation controls in the toolbar to move between pages and apply edits, annotations, or color changes to any page in your document.

What formats are supported and what can I export?

Upload PDF, PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP, or TIFF files up to 50 MB. Export your finished document as PDF, PNG, or JPG in full quality — with zero watermarks, zero compression artifacts.

Are my uploaded PDF files stored on your servers?

No. All editing happens locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your files never leave your device. When you close the tab, nothing is retained — your documents are completely private.

What's the difference between the PDF Editor and the Image Editor?

The PDF Editor is built specifically for PDF files with multi-page navigation and PDF-native text extraction. The Image Editor works on raster images (PNG, JPG, screenshots) with the same OCR, color picker, and AI tools. Both support click-to-edit text, AI inpainting, color picker, background changer, and watermark-free export.
