Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free online voice to text converter.

Is this voice to text tool completely free?

Yes — Flarixo AI's voice to text converter is 100% free with no sign-up or credit card required. Just upload and transcribe.

What audio formats are supported?

You can upload MP3, WAV, OGG, WEBM, M4A, and FLAC audio files for transcription.

What languages can be transcribed?

The tool supports over 80 languages including English, Spanish, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, French, German, Chinese, and many more. Select a language manually or use auto-detect.

How accurate is the auto-detect language feature?

Auto-detect uses AI to identify the spoken language and shows a confidence score alongside the result. Selecting the correct language manually can improve accuracy further.

Is there a file size limit?

Files up to 25 MB transcribe quickly. Larger files are still accepted but may take longer to process.

Are my uploaded audio files stored on your servers?

No. Files are processed securely and immediately discarded after transcription. We never store, share, or access your files.
