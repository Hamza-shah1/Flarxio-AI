FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about editing images and screenshots online with Flarixo AI.

Can I edit text in an image like a Word document?

Yes — that's exactly what Flarixo AI is built for. Our AI OCR engine detects every text region in your image and makes them clickable. When you click a word, you can retype it and the editor automatically inherits the original font family, size, weight, and color. The result looks completely native — no mismatched overlays.

Is sign-in required to use the image editor?

Yes — a free Flarixo AI account is required. Sign-in unlocks the full editor including OCR text detection, the color picker, background color tool, export without watermarks, and undo/redo history. Creating an account takes under 30 seconds and requires no credit card.

How does the color picker work?

Flarixo AI includes a native browser color picker with full HEX and HSL support — that's over 16 million colors. You can set stroke color (for drawing and text), fill color (for shapes and the fill tool), and a separate background color for the canvas.

Can I change the background color of an image?

Yes. The right panel has a dedicated Background section. Pick any color from the color picker and click Apply Background to flood-fill the canvas background. Great for changing white or transparent backgrounds to match a brand color.

Does it match the original font when I edit text?

Yes. When OCR detects a text region, it reads the apparent font size, weight, and style. When you click to edit, the editor pre-fills those properties so your new text matches the original as closely as possible.

What image formats are supported?

The editor accepts PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, TIFF, and TIF files up to 50 MB each. Export is available in PNG, JPG, and WebP formats.

Are my images stored on Flarixo servers?

No. All editing happens securely in your browser session. Your uploaded images are never stored on Flarixo servers after your session ends, and nothing is shared with third parties.

What's the difference between the Image Editor and the PDF Editor?

The Image Editor uses OCR on raster images (PNG, JPG, screenshots) and provides drawing, text, annotation, and editing tools. The PDF Editor is designed specifically for PDF documents with multi-page navigation and PDF-native text extraction. Both support click-to-edit text, color picker, background changer, and watermark-free export.
