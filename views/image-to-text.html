Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free image to text (OCR) converter.

Is this image to text tool completely free?

Yes — Flarixo AI's image to text (OCR) tool is 100% free with no sign-up or credit card required. Just upload and extract.

What image formats are supported for text extraction?

You can extract text from JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and most common image formats, including screenshots and scanned documents.

Can I extract text from only part of an image?

Yes. Switch to "Select Area" mode and draw a box around the specific text you want, or stay in "Extract All Text" mode to convert the whole image at once.

Does the OCR tool support handwriting?

The tool is optimized for printed and typed text such as documents, screenshots, and signage. Clear handwriting may also be recognized, though accuracy can vary.

Are my uploaded images stored on your servers?

No. Images are processed securely in memory and immediately discarded after text extraction. We never store, share, or access your files.

Can I copy or download the extracted text?

Yes — after extraction you can copy the text to your clipboard with one click, or download it as a plain .txt file.
