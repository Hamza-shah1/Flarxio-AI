Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about converting images to PDF with Flarixo AI.

How do I convert an image to PDF?

Upload your JPG, PNG, or WEBP image, reorder or remove images as needed, then click Convert to PDF. Your file downloads in seconds — no software or sign-up needed.

Can I reorder images before converting?

Yes. After uploading, simply drag the thumbnails into the order you want. The PDF pages will follow that exact sequence. On mobile, a long-press activates the drag handle.

Can I delete a specific image before converting?

Yes. Click the red × button on any thumbnail to remove that image from the batch. The remaining images stay in place and page numbers update automatically.

Can I merge multiple images into one PDF?

Yes. Select multiple files at once or drag several images onto the upload area. All images are combined into a single PDF in the order you set.

Is this image to PDF converter completely free?

Yes, 100% free. No account, no credit card, and no watermark on the output PDF.

What image formats are supported?

The tool supports JPG (JPEG), PNG, and WEBP image formats. The output is always a standard PDF compatible with all devices.

Does this work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. The tool works in any modern mobile browser — no app download required. Drag-to-reorder is fully supported on touch devices.

Are my images kept private?

Yes. Your images are uploaded securely, used only to generate the PDF, then deleted from our servers immediately. We never store or share your files.

What quality is the output PDF?

The PDF preserves your original image resolution — no compression, no quality loss. Each image is placed on its own page, sized to fit perfectly.
