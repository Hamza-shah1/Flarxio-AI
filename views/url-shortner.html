Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Flarixo AI's free URL shortener.

Is this URL shortener completely free?

Yes — Flarixo AI's URL shortener is 100% free with no sign-up or credit card required. Just paste your URL and shorten it instantly.

Are my shortened links permanent — do they expire?

Short links created with Flarixo AI are permanent and never expire. You can safely share them in emails, social posts, or printed materials without worrying about broken links in the future.

How does Flarixo AI scan links for malware and phishing?

Every URL goes through a multi-layer security pipeline: structural validation, suspicious keyword detection, homoglyph brand-impersonation checks, domain entropy analysis, Google Safe Browsing verification, and redirect chain inspection. If any check fails, the short link is not created — protecting both you and anyone who clicks your link.

Can I track how many times my short link is clicked?

Yes. Every short link includes click analytics so you can monitor traffic, measure engagement, and understand how your link is performing over time.

Can I create a custom alias for my short link?

Yes. Instead of a random string, you can choose a custom alias — for example, flarixo.ai/s/my-brand — to make your short link memorable and on-brand. Custom aliases are available at no cost.

Is a QR code automatically generated for my link?

Yes. A downloadable QR code is generated for every short link. This is ideal for print campaigns, product packaging, business cards, and any situation where you want to bridge physical and digital content.

What is the maximum URL length I can shorten?

Flarixo AI supports URLs up to 2,048 characters — well beyond the length of any typical web URL, including those with long query parameters or tracking codes.
